PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Professional Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of
January 15, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

  PROVIDER:  Alpine Solutions Group, LLC
             800 Innovation Blvd, Austin, TX 78701
             ("Provider")

  CLIENT:    Woodgrove Bank, N.A.
             350 Banking Center Drive, Chicago, IL 60601
             ("Client")

Collectively referred to as the "Parties."

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

Provider shall deliver the following services to Client:

  1.1 Design and implement a customer identity and access management
      (CIAM) platform for Client's digital banking channels.
  1.2 Integrate CIAM with Client's existing core banking system,
      mobile banking application, and online portal.
  1.3 Implement multi-factor authentication (MFA), adaptive risk-based
      authentication, and passwordless sign-in capabilities.
  1.4 Conduct security testing, including penetration testing and
      vulnerability assessment of the implemented solution.
  1.5 Provide training to Client's IT operations team (minimum 24 hours).

2. CONTRACT VALUE AND PAYMENT TERMS

  2.1 Total Contract Value: $650,000 USD
  2.2 Payment Schedule:
      - 20% ($130,000) upon execution of this Agreement
      - 30% ($195,000) upon completion of Phase 1 (Design & Architecture)
      - 30% ($195,000) upon completion of Phase 2 (Implementation & Testing)
      - 20% ($130,000) upon final acceptance and go-live
  2.3 Invoices are due Net 30 from date of invoice.
  2.4 Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. TERM AND EXPIRATION

  3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue
      for a period of twelve (12) months, expiring on January 14, 2027.
  3.2 The Parties may extend this Agreement by mutual written consent
      for additional six (6) month periods.

4. KEY CLAUSES

  4.1 CONFIDENTIALITY
      Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all
      Confidential Information received from the other Party for a
      period of five (5) years following disclosure. Confidential
      Information includes, but is not limited to, technical data,
      trade secrets, customer lists, and financial information.

  4.2 INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
      All pre-existing intellectual property shall remain the property
      of its respective owner. Work product created specifically for
      Client under this Agreement ("Deliverables") shall be owned by
      Client upon full payment. Provider retains rights to its
      pre-existing tools, frameworks, and methodologies.

  4.3 INDEMNIFICATION
      Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless Client from any
      third-party claims arising from Provider's negligence or willful
      misconduct in performing the Services. Client shall indemnify
      Provider from claims arising from Client's use of Deliverables
      in violation of applicable law.

  4.4 LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
      Neither Party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall
      exceed the total Contract Value ($650,000). Neither Party shall
      be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive
      damages, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

  4.5 DATA PROTECTION
      Provider shall comply with all applicable data protection laws,
      including GLBA, CCPA, and any applicable state privacy laws.
      Provider shall implement appropriate technical and organizational
      measures to protect Client's customer data.

5. TERMINATION CONDITIONS

  5.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon
      sixty (60) days' written notice.
  5.2 Either Party may terminate immediately upon material breach by
      the other Party, provided the breaching Party has been given
      thirty (30) days' written notice and opportunity to cure.
  5.3 Client may terminate immediately if Provider undergoes a change
      of control, bankruptcy, or insolvency.
  5.4 Upon termination, Provider shall deliver all work-in-progress
      and Deliverables to Client within fifteen (15) business days.

6. OBLIGATIONS

  Provider Obligations:
    - Assign a dedicated project manager and technical lead
    - Provide weekly status reports and monthly executive summaries
    - Maintain professional liability insurance of at least $2,000,000
    - Ensure all personnel pass background checks
    - Comply with Client's information security policies

  Client Obligations:
    - Provide timely access to systems, environments, and personnel
    - Designate a project sponsor and technical point of contact
    - Review and provide feedback on deliverables within 10 business days
    - Provide test data and test environments
    - Execute change orders for scope modifications

7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with
the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of
laws provisions. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding
arbitration in Chicago, Illinois.
