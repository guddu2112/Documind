REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Enterprise Data Analytics Platform
RFP-2026-0087

Issued by: Contoso Financial Services, Ltd.
Date: February 1, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Contoso Financial Services, Ltd. ("Contoso") invites qualified technology
firms to submit proposals for the design, implementation, and deployment
of an enterprise-wide data analytics platform. The platform shall enable
real-time reporting, predictive analytics, and regulatory compliance
reporting across all business units.

2. ISSUING ORGANIZATION

Contoso Financial Services, Ltd.
500 Financial Plaza
New York, NY 10004
Contact: James Rodriguez, Chief Data Officer

3. SUBMISSION DEADLINE

Proposals must be received by: March 28, 2026, 11:59 PM Eastern Time.

4. SCOPE OF WORK

  a) Design a unified data lakehouse architecture consolidating data
     from 12 source systems (core banking, CRM, trading, risk, compliance).
  b) Implement real-time data ingestion pipelines processing approximately
     50 million events per day.
  c) Build a self-service analytics layer with governed data catalog.
  d) Create 25 executive dashboards and 40 operational reports.
  e) Implement ML model serving infrastructure for fraud detection and
     credit scoring models.
  f) Ensure SOX, PCI-DSS, and GDPR compliance throughout.

5. REQUIREMENTS

  R1. Platform must support sub-second query response for dashboards.
  R2. Data retention policy: 7 years for transactional data.
  R3. Role-based access control with column-level security.
  R4. Automated data quality checks with alerting.
  R5. Support for both batch (hourly) and streaming (real-time) pipelines.
  R6. Disaster recovery with RPO < 1 hour and RTO < 4 hours.

6. EVALUATION CRITERIA

  Technical Architecture & Scalability    30%
  Financial Services Domain Experience    25%
  Data Governance & Compliance            20%
  Cost Effectiveness                      15%
  Support & SLA Commitments               10%

7. BUDGET RANGE

$2,000,000 to $3,500,000 USD for initial implementation.
Ongoing annual support budget: $400,000 to $600,000 USD.

8. CONTRACT DURATION

24-month implementation phase followed by a 36-month managed services
agreement.
